Common questions

Why am I seeing a demo library with fictional albums?

Flipside uses a demo library until it can reach your Apple Music library. Tap "Connect Apple Music" (in the Library screen or the "..." menu) and allow access when iOS asks. You can switch back to the demo library at any time.

I turned off access by mistake. How do I turn it back on?

Open the iOS Settings app, scroll to Flipside, and allow Media & Apple Music. Then reopen Flipside and tap "Connect Apple Music".

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

To stream music from the Apple Music catalog, yes. Flipside plays your music through Apple's own services, so it follows the same rules as Apple Music itself. Without a subscription you can still try every deck with the built-in demo library.

How do the free previews work?

Tap any locked deck or finish to see it live, with your own music, for 30 seconds. When the time is up, an offer to unlock it appears after the current song finishes, never in the middle of one. You never need to buy anything to keep using the free CD deck and Champagne finish.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in Flipside (from the "..." menu) and tap "Restore Purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Purchases also come back automatically on a new device signed in to that Apple ID.

What is the Collector's Set?

A one-time purchase that unlocks every deck and every finish, including any added in the future. If you already own some items, you can still buy it.

Does the 33/45 switch change my music?

No. It only changes how fast the record spins on screen. Your music always plays at its normal speed and pitch.

The effects feel too busy, or I want it quieter.

In Settings you can turn off sound effects, vinyl crackle and tape hiss, and haptics separately. If you turn on Reduce Motion in iOS Settings, discs stay still and the tilt shine is switched off. Low Power Mode automatically lowers effect quality.

The cassette or vinyl library looks empty.

Each deck shows the same library on its own kind of shelf. If a shelf looks empty, check which tab is selected (Albums, Playlists, Artists, Genres, Recently Added, or the binder tabs). Favourites only shows albums you have hearted in Flipside.

Can I request a feature or report a bug?

Please do. Email colin@circa.ooo.
