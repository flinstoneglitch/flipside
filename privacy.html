Flipside

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The short version: Flipside does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. Your music and listening stay on your device.

This policy explains how the Flipside app for iPhone ("Flipside", "the app") handles information. Flipside is made by Colin Oreilly ("I", "me").

Information I collect

None. I don't run a server for the app, and the app doesn't send any information about you or your use of it to me or to anyone else. I don't use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or any other third-party SDKs.

Apple Music and your library

Flipside plays your music through Apple's MusicKit framework. When you choose to connect, iOS asks for your permission to access your media and Apple Music library. If you allow it, Flipside reads your albums, playlists, artists and artwork on your device so it can show them on the decks and play them. This information is handled by iOS and MusicKit, stays on your device, and is never sent to me. You can turn access off at any time in iOS Settings, under Flipside. Playing streamed catalog music requires your own active Apple Music subscription, which is between you and Apple.

If you don't connect Apple Music, Flipside uses a built-in demo library of fictional albums. Nothing about it leaves your device.

Purchases

Flipside offers optional one-time in-app purchases (decks, finishes and a Collector's Set) and optional tips. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. I never see your payment details, name, Apple ID or email address. Flipside checks what you own directly with Apple on your device, so your purchases work offline and can be restored with "Restore Purchases".

Data stored on your device

Flipside saves a few small things locally on your device so the app remembers how you like it:

  • Your settings (sound effects, haptics, selected deck and finish, volume knob position)
  • Which albums you have marked as favourites inside the app
  • A simple count of listening sessions, used only to decide when it is a reasonable moment to ask for an App Store rating
  • A cached list of which purchases you own

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Motion data

Flipside reads your device's motion sensors while the app is open so the shine on the CD and vinyl moves as you tilt your phone. This is used only to draw the effect on screen. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and it is switched off if you enable Reduce Motion.

Ratings

Flipside may occasionally show Apple's standard rating prompt. Whether you rate the app is entirely up to you, and nothing in the app depends on it. Rating prompts are controlled by iOS and Apple.

Children

Flipside doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Third parties

The only third-party services involved are Apple's own: MusicKit (playback and library access), StoreKit and the App Store (purchases and ratings). Their handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If I ever change how Flipside handles information, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
